In the DriverPass Project, we designed a system for the owner of DriverPass. The system was a comprehensive educational system for students looking to pass their Department of Motor Vehicles driving test. This includes Scheduling, recording and administering tests, as well as functionality for staff including maintenance and downloading reports. I am especially proud of the UML diagrams I made for this project. If I was to revise anything I think I could make the technical requirements look even more professional. In this Project we collected the user’s needs through an interview transcript. I then made a series of notes of the key requirements such as instructor scheduling, generated reports and staff privileges. I then implemented these requests in the using documentation. This displays the importance of collecting user requirements and designing around them. It also displays the importance of interviews in designing software, in the future I would use a combination of interviews and written documentation to collect user requirements. I would then use these to generate high level design documents such as activity diagrams and decompose them into lower level implementations and designs such as entity relationship diagrams.
